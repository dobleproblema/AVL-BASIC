The Lantern Path - original audio for g-dungeon.bas

A short ambient music loop with footsteps, key and gate sounds, and a
victory fanfare. All music and effects were composed and synthesized for
this demo; no third-party recordings, samples or melodies are used.

Keep all WAV files in samples/assets/audio/dungeon when copying the demo.
