Copper Drive - original music for g-old-school.bas
=================================================

A 16-bar, 132 BPM loop composed and synthesized for this demo. The stereo
16-bit PCM WAV is 29.091 seconds long at 44100 Hz. The composition and all
sounds are original; no third-party recordings, samples or melodies are used.

Keep copper-drive.wav in samples/assets/audio/old-school when copying the demo.
