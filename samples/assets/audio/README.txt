chime.wav is an original, procedurally generated sample for s-audio.bas.
It contains a 0.4-second decaying C-major chime (523.25, 659.25 and 783.99 Hz),
stored as mono 16-bit PCM at 22050 Hz. It contains no third-party recordings.

crystal-siege/ contains the five original, procedurally generated WAV files
used by g-crystal.bas and g-crystal-classic.bas: silent-prism-loop.wav,
launch.wav, impact-glass.wav, fracture.wav, and victory.wav.
They use 16-bit PCM at 44100 Hz and contain no third-party recordings.
